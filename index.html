DEEP WIRING

Deep Wiring Factory

Deep Wiring Factory is a private desktop tool used by the owner of the Deep Wiring YouTube channel to prepare Shorts and long-form videos. It runs on the owner’s computer and tracks production jobs, video quality checks and planned releases.

The owner can connect the desktop tool to YouTube to verify the selected channel, upload a prepared video, schedule an approved release and retrieve statistics for videos uploaded through the tool.

This public site describes the tool. It has no sign-in, uploads or access to the private dashboard.
